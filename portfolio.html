Bike Shack AI

Dawn development store showcasing two flagship features built with Claude Code through an AI-assisted, human-reviewed workflow: a scroll-driven helmet video that pins the page and scrubs with your scroll (timed tooltips, reduced-motion fallback), and a custom collection grid with sculpted L-shaped cards in pure CSS—hover image reveal, native filtering and sorting. The Sharp Bean

A development store built on Shopify's Dawn theme through an AI-assisted, human-reviewed workflow. The custom product page pairs a brand-styled size and grind picker with inventory-aware bag logic—options grey out or sell out based on real stock—plus a live total and a free-shipping bar that appears at a set number of bags or cart value (displayed in the theme, enforced by Shopify's native discounts). Backed by a custom, contrast-checked color system and accessibility aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA. Documented outcome: inventory logic verified across 96 size-and-grind variants (8 products) over six recorded QA rounds.
