How I Work

A transparent, customer-first process designed to ensure we're the right fit for your project.

1

Email Consultation

Reach out via email with a brief overview of your store, goals, and challenges. I'll review your situation and suggest a time for our call.

2

30-Minute Proton Meet Call

I'll send you a private Proton Meet room link. We'll dive deep into your requirements, discuss your vision, and explore how I can help.

3

Custom Proposal

Based on our conversation, I'll send a detailed proposal with timeline, scope, and investment. No pressure—if we're not the right fit, there's zero cost.

100% Risk-Free: The consultation and proposal are completely free. If we can't meet your expectations or align on requirements, there's no obligation and no cost to you. I put you first.

Workflow

A documented, human-reviewed process keeps client themes connected to GitHub for safe, repeatable delivery.

  1. 1) Scope & Plan

    Every feature is scoped and planned before any code is written— requirements, approach, and acceptance criteria agreed up front.

  2. 2) Build & Review

    Built with AI assistance, then human-reviewed—pull requests for every change, preview branches, and clear release notes.

  3. 3) QA & Ship

    Tested in a development environment with QA sign-off before anything reaches your live store—backups, rollback plan, and full version history included.
