Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site and submit the contact form.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, company, budget range, and message. We may also collect basic technical information such as your IP address and browser details for security and abuse prevention purposes.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, provide requested services information, and follow up on potential projects. We also use technical information to help prevent spam and abuse of the contact form.

Contact management system

Contact form submissions may be stored in Mautic (our contact management system) for lead management and follow-up purposes. Only the minimum information necessary to respond and manage your inquiry is stored. We do not share your information with third parties without your consent.

Data retention

Contact submissions may be retained as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the email address provided on the Contact page.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice on this website. Continued use of the site after such updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
