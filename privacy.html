Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site and contact us by email.

Information we collect

This site does not use a web contact form. When you reach out using the email link on the Contact page, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your name, email address, company, budget range, and project details. Our hosting provider may also log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser/request details for security and reliability.

How we use your information

We use the information you send to respond to your inquiry, provide requested information about our services, and follow up on potential projects. Technical information logged by our host is used to help keep the site secure and available.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies, and we do not track your activity across other websites or sell your information.

Hosting and service providers

This site is served as static files by our web hosting provider, which may process basic technical data (such as IP addresses and server request logs) to deliver, secure, and maintain the site. Inquiries are sent directly to us by email using the address on the Contact page; we do not use a third-party form-processing service. We use service providers solely to operate the website and respond to inquiries, and we do not sell or rent your information.

Data retention

Email inquiries may be retained as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your submitted information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the email address provided on the Contact page.

California and EU/UK privacy rights

If you are a California resident or located in the EU or UK: we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. Where required, our basis for processing your inquiry is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to you. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us using the email on the Contact page.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice on this website. Continued use of the site after such updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
